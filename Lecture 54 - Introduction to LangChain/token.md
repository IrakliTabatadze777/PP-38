Here is a simple example:

Take the sentence: 
> **"I love learning about AI!"**

An AI model doesn't read whole words the way humans do; it breaks the text down into tokens like this:

* **Token 1:** `I`
* **Token 2:** ` love`
* **Token 3:** ` learning`
* **Token 4:** ` about`
* **Token 5:** ` AI`
* **Token 6:** `!`

### Sub-word Example
Tokens aren't always full words. Common words might be a single token, but longer or uncommon words get broken into smaller pieces:

The word **"Unbelievable"** might be broken into 3 tokens:
* `Un` + `believ` + `able`

### A quick rule of thumb for English:
* **1 token** $\approx$ **4 characters** or **0.75 words**
* **100 tokens** $\approx$ **75 words**
