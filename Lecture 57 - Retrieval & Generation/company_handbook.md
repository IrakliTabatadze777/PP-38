# Company Handbook

## Working Hours

Our core working hours are 10am to 4pm local time, Monday through Friday.
Outside of core hours, employees are free to structure their day as they see
fit, as long as deliverables are met and core-hour meetings are attended.
Remote work is fully supported; there is no requirement to be in an office on
any particular day. Teams that prefer more structure can agree on their own
overlap hours, but the company-wide baseline is the 10am-4pm core window.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work from home, a coworking space, or any other location with
a stable internet connection, on a fully permanent basis if they choose.
There is no minimum number of in-office days required. Employees who live
within commuting distance of a company office are welcome to use it any time
it's open, but attendance is never tracked and never factors into
performance reviews. Remote employees are expected to be reachable during
their team's agreed overlap hours and to keep their calendar accurate so
teammates in other time zones can schedule meetings without back-and-forth.
The company covers a one-time home office setup stipend (see Equipment
Policy) and a monthly stipend toward internet and coworking costs for fully
remote employees, processed automatically through payroll each month.

## Vacation Policy

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per year, prorated for
partial years of employment. Vacation requests should be submitted through
the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for anything longer than three
consecutive days. Unused vacation days roll over up to a maximum of 5 days
into the following calendar year; anything beyond that is forfeited.
Vacation accrual continues during approved leave of any kind, and there is
no cap on how many vacation days can be taken consecutively, subject to
manager approval and adequate team coverage during the absence.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from vacation
allowance and not requiring advance notice. For absences longer than three
consecutive sick days, a note from a healthcare provider may be requested by
HR. Sick days do not roll over between calendar years and are not paid out
upon departure. Employees who are feeling unwell are strongly encouraged to
stay home and work remotely if able, or take the day fully off, rather than
coming into an office space.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business-related expenses (travel, client meals, conference tickets, and
necessary equipment) are reimbursable with a submitted receipt. Reimbursement
requests must be filed within 60 days of the expense being incurred, through
the finance team's expense portal. Expenses over $500 require manager
pre-approval before the purchase is made, not after. Reimbursements are
typically processed within 10 business days of a correctly submitted
request, and paid out in the next regular payroll cycle.

## Travel Policy

Business travel should be booked at least two weeks in advance through the
company's approved travel portal whenever possible, to take advantage of
negotiated corporate rates. Economy class is standard for flights under six
hours; flights longer than six hours may be booked in premium economy.
Ground transportation, reasonable meals, and standard baggage fees are
reimbursable under the Expense Reimbursement policy above. Employees
traveling internationally for business should notify IT in advance so a
travel-mode security profile can be enabled on company devices.

## Equipment Policy

New employees receive a laptop, monitor, and any reasonable peripherals
needed to do their job, selected from the standard equipment catalog.
Equipment is company property and must be returned upon departure. Requests
for non-standard equipment (e.g., a second monitor, specialized software
licenses) go through the employee's manager for approval. Fully remote
employees also receive a one-time $500 home office setup stipend, usable for
a desk, chair, monitor arm, or similar, reimbursed through the standard
expense process within the first 90 days of employment.

## IT and Security Policy

All company devices must have full-disk encryption and the standard
endpoint security agent installed before being used for company work.
Multi-factor authentication is required on all accounts that support it,
without exception. Company data should never be stored on personal, unmanaged
devices. Suspected phishing emails or security incidents should be reported
immediately to the security team's dedicated channel rather than investigated
personally. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 24 hours so they
can be remotely wiped.

## Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

Employees may have access to confidential company, client, or partner
information in the course of their work, and are expected to protect it both
during and after their employment. Work product created during employment
using company resources or time belongs to the company, consistent with the
employment agreement signed at hiring. Employees are free to discuss their
own general skills and experience publicly, but should not share
proprietary code, internal metrics, or unreleased product details outside
the company without explicit approval.

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with
respect and professionalism. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of
any kind are not tolerated and should be reported to HR immediately. Concerns
can be raised confidentially, and retaliation against anyone who raises a
concern in good faith is itself a violation of this policy. Reports can be
made directly to HR, to any manager, or through the anonymous ethics hotline
listed on the internal HR portal.

## Diversity and Inclusion

The company is committed to building a workplace where people of all
backgrounds can do their best work. Hiring, promotion, and compensation
decisions are made based on skills, experience, and performance, and are
periodically audited for consistency across demographic groups. Employee
resource groups are open to anyone interested and receive a small annual
budget for events, coordinated through HR.

## Workplace Safety

Any workplace injury, no matter how minor, should be reported to HR within
24 hours using the incident report form on the HR portal. Employees working
from a company office are expected to familiarize themselves with the
building's emergency exits and assembly point, covered during new-hire
orientation. Ergonomic equipment assessments are available on request for
both office-based and remote employees.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Reviews
combine self-assessment, manager feedback, and peer feedback from two to
three colleagues chosen by the employee. Compensation changes, when they
happen, are typically communicated within two weeks of the review cycle's
completion. New employees receive an additional informal check-in at the
90-day mark, separate from the standard cycle, to surface any early
onboarding issues.

## Compensation and Bonuses

Salaries are reviewed annually as part of the December performance review
cycle, though off-cycle adjustments can happen for significant role changes.
Eligible employees may receive an annual performance bonus, targeted as a
percentage of base salary and dependent on both individual and company
performance; specific targets are communicated during onboarding and any
subsequent role change. Referral bonuses are paid out for successful hires
referred by current employees, 90 days after the new hire's start date.

## Professional Development

Each employee has an annual professional development budget for courses,
books, conferences, or certifications relevant to their role, usable at
their own discretion without requiring a specific business justification
beyond general relevance. Unused budget does not roll over to the following
year. Employees attending external conferences on company time should share
a brief summary of key takeaways with their team afterward.

## Company Holidays

The company observes 10 paid public holidays per year, published on the HR
portal at the start of each calendar year and varying slightly by country of
employment to reflect local public holidays. Employees in regions where a
locally observed holiday isn't on the standard company list may swap it for
one of their vacation days with manager approval.

## Meeting Culture

Meetings should have a written agenda shared in advance and a clear owner
responsible for keeping them on time. Any meeting that could be replaced by
a written update should be. Recurring meetings are reviewed quarterly by
their owner to confirm they still need to exist. Recording meetings is
allowed with the consent of all attendees, primarily to let colleagues in
other time zones catch up asynchronously rather than requiring live
attendance.

## Internal Communication Channels

Time-sensitive questions should go through the team chat rather than email,
which is reserved for external communication and formal records. Each team
maintains a public channel for updates that anyone in the company can follow.
Company-wide announcements are posted to the general announcements channel
and are also summarized in a weekly digest email sent every Friday.

## Offboarding Process

Departing employees should coordinate a knowledge-transfer plan with their
manager covering their last two weeks of employment. All company equipment
must be returned within 5 business days of the last day of employment,
using the prepaid shipping label provided by IT. Final pay, including any
unused vacation payout, is processed in the next regular payroll cycle
following departure, consistent with local employment law.
