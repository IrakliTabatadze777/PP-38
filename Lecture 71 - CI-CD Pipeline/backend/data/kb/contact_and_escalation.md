# Contact and Escalation

## Support hours

Our support team is available Monday to Friday from 9 am to 6 pm Mountain Time, excluding public holidays. Messages sent outside these hours are answered on the next business day.

## How to reach us

You can chat with the support assistant at any time, or email support@northpeak.example. Include your order number and the email address on the order, so we can find your order without delays.

## When a person takes over

The assistant can answer questions about our policies and look up your orders. A member of our support team takes over when you ask for a person, when your question is not covered by our policies, when the assistant cannot find your order after two tries, when your request needs a judgment call such as a return in the grace period, or when you mention a legal complaint or a chargeback.

## Refund and replacement decisions

The assistant can prepare a refund or a replacement, but a member of our support team always decides. Until that decision is made, the request is not confirmed.

## What happens when you ask for a person

We open a support ticket with a short summary of your conversation, so you do not have to repeat yourself. A support team member replies by email, usually within one business day. Tickets about legal complaints or chargebacks are marked high priority.

## Feedback and complaints

We welcome feedback about products and service. Send it to support@northpeak.example, and we pass it to the right team.
