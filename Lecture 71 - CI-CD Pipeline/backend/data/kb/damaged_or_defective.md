# Damaged or Defective Items

## What counts as damaged or defective

An item is damaged if it was harmed in transit, for example a torn box with a crushed tent pole inside. An item is defective if it does not work as designed when it arrives, for example a jacket with a broken zipper, a backpack with a strap that comes off, or boots with a sole that separates. Wear and tear from normal use does not count.

## Refund for damaged or defective items

If the item was delivered 30 days ago or less, you are entitled to a refund of the amount you paid plus the original shipping cost. This applies even to final sale items. You do not have to return the item in its original packaging in this case.

## Grace period

If the item was delivered 31 to 37 days ago, your request goes to a member of our support team, who decides on it as an exception. Please explain what is wrong and when you noticed it.

## Evidence we may ask for

Photos of the damage and of the outer packaging help us decide quickly. For defects, tell us what happened and how long you used the item. We may ask for photos, but we do not require a return of the item before the refund is reviewed.

## Replacement instead of a refund

If you prefer a replacement over a refund, tell us. We can send the same item again when it is in stock. A replacement is also reviewed by a member of our support team before it is created.

## After 37 days

More than 37 days after delivery, damage in transit is no longer covered by this policy. A manufacturing defect may still be covered by the warranty. See the warranty page for how long each product category is covered.
