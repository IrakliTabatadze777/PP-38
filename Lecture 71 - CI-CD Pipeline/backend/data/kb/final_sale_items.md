# Final Sale Items

## What final sale means

Some items are sold at a reduced price and marked as final sale on the product page and in your order. Final sale items cannot be returned or refunded because you changed your mind, the size does not fit or the item does not match what you expected. This applies for as long as you own the item, no matter how recently it was delivered.

## Which items are final sale

Final sale items are clearance items, discontinued items and some seasonal accessories. The product page and your order confirmation show whether an item is final sale. If you are unsure, ask us before you buy.

## Exception for damaged or defective items

A final sale item that arrives damaged or defective can be refunded. The same rules apply as for any other item: the item must have been delivered 30 days ago or less to qualify for a full refund including the original shipping cost. Days 31 to 37 go to a member of our support team. After that, only the warranty applies where the product category has one.

## Orders with several items

If one order contains both regular and final sale items, only the regular items can be returned for any reason. The final sale items stay with the order unless they are damaged or defective.

## Exchanges

Final sale items cannot be exchanged for a different size or colour either. A replacement is possible only when the item arrived damaged or defective, or when we sent the wrong item.
