# Order Status FAQ

## What do the order statuses mean?

An order is always in one of four statuses: placed, shipped, delivered or cancelled.

## Placed

Your order was received and paid for, and is being picked and packed at our warehouse. Orders usually leave the warehouse the same business day or the next one. You can still cancel an order in this status by contacting us.

## Shipped

Your order has left the warehouse and is with the carrier. You received a tracking number by email. We cannot cancel a shipped order, but you can return it after it is delivered. See the returns policy for the 30 day window.

## Delivered

The carrier marked the order as delivered. The delivery date is the day the return window starts. If tracking says delivered but you do not have the package, contact us and we will start a search with the carrier.

## Cancelled

The order was cancelled and will not be shipped. If you paid, the money goes back to the original payment method.

## Can I return or get a refund for an order that has not been delivered?

No. A return or refund can only be processed after the order is delivered. If the order is still placed, you can ask us to cancel it. If it has shipped, wait for delivery and then start a return if you need one.

## How do I check my order?

Contact support with your order number and the email on the order. We need both to show you the order details.
