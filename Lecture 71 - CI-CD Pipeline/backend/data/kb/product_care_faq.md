# Product Care FAQ

## How do I wash a waterproof jacket?

Close all zippers and fasteners, and wash the jacket on a gentle cycle in cool water with a technical wash product. Do not use fabric softener or bleach, because they damage the waterproof layer. Rinse twice. Tumble dry on low heat for 20 minutes to help the water-repellent finish recover.

## How do I restore waterproofing?

When water stops beading on the surface, wash the jacket and then apply a spray-on or wash-in water repellent following the product instructions. Heat from a tumble dryer on low sets the treatment.

## How do I clean and dry hiking boots?

Brush off dirt and rinse with lukewarm water. Remove the insoles and laces and let the boots dry at room temperature, away from radiators and direct sunlight, because strong heat can crack leather and weaken glue. Stuff them with newspaper to speed up drying. Treat leather boots with a wax or conditioner a few times a season.

## How should I store a tent?

Always dry a tent completely before storing it. A damp tent grows mould within days. Store it loosely in a large breathable bag rather than tightly compressed, in a cool, dry place. Wipe the poles and check the zipper before you pack it away.

## How do I clean a tent?

Use lukewarm water and a soft sponge. Do not machine wash a tent or use detergent, because both damage the coating. Let it dry fully before storing.

## How do I care for a down jacket?

Wash down jackets in a front-loading machine with down wash on a gentle cycle. Dry on low heat with clean tennis balls to break up clumps and restore loft.

## How do I care for a backpack?

Empty the pack, brush out dirt and wipe it with a damp cloth. Do not machine wash a backpack, because it damages the frame and coating. Let it air dry open.
