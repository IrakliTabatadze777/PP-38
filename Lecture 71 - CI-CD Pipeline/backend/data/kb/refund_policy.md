# Refund Policy

## Refund amounts

A refund is never more than the amount you actually paid for the item. If you used a discount, you are refunded the discounted price, not the list price. On an ordinary return the original shipping cost is not included. If the item arrived damaged or defective, the refund also includes the original shipping cost. Shipping is refunded once per order, even when several items from the same order are returned.

## Refund method and timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method. We cannot send a refund to a different card or account. After a refund is approved, it usually shows up within 5 to 7 business days. The exact time depends on your bank or card issuer.

## Refunds are reviewed by our team

Every refund is checked and approved by a member of our support team before any money is sent. Until that review is done, the refund is not confirmed. You will be told the result as soon as it is decided. Refunds over 200 USD are marked as high-value and receive an additional check, which can take a little longer.

## One refund per item

Each order item can be refunded only once. If a refund was already issued for an item, a second request for the same item is declined automatically. If you think a refund is missing or wrong, contact support and we will look at the order with you.

## Verifying your identity

We only share order information after the order number and the email address on the order both match. If they do not match, we cannot look up the order or process a refund.

## Refund status

If more than 7 business days have passed since your refund was approved and you still do not see it, contact your bank first, because the time to post a refund depends on the bank. If the bank cannot find it, contact us and we will check the refund on our side.
