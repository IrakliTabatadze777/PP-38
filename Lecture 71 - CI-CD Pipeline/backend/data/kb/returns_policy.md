# Returns Policy

## Return window

You can return an item within 30 days of the day it was delivered. The 30 days are counted from the delivery date shown on your order, not from the day you placed the order.

## Condition of returned items

Items must be unused, clean and in their original packaging, with all tags attached. Boots that have been worn outdoors, tents that have been pitched and jackets with signs of wear do not meet this condition. If you tried an item on indoors and it was not worn outside, it can still be returned.

## Returns after the 30 days

If your item was delivered 31 to 37 days ago, you are in our grace period. We cannot approve these returns automatically. A member of our support team reviews each one and decides, so please contact us and explain why the return is late. After 37 days we can no longer accept returns of items that are not damaged or defective. Items that arrive damaged or break because of a manufacturing defect are covered separately by our damaged and defective item policy and our warranty.

## Return shipping costs

We do not refund the original shipping cost on ordinary returns, for example when you changed your mind or the size did not fit. The only exception is an item that arrived damaged or defective, where the original shipping cost is refunded as well.

## How to start a return

Contact support with your order number and the email address on the order. We need both to confirm the order is yours before we can show or change anything. Tell us which item you want to return and why. We check whether the item qualifies and tell you the next step. Refunds are issued after the return is approved. See the refund policy for amounts and timing.

## Final sale items

Items marked as final sale cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective. See the final sale policy for details.
