# Shipping Policy

## Shipping methods and delivery estimates

Standard shipping arrives in 3 to 5 business days after your order leaves our warehouse. Express shipping arrives in 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 2 pm Mountain Time on a business day usually leave the warehouse the same day. Delivery estimates are not guarantees, and busy periods such as the holidays can add a day or two.

## Shipping costs

Shipping is charged at checkout and depends on the size and weight of the order. The shipping cost is shown on your order. On an ordinary return the shipping cost is not refunded. If an item arrives damaged or defective, the original shipping cost is refunded together with the item.

## Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States and Canada. We deliver to PO boxes and APO or FPO addresses with Standard shipping only. Express shipping needs a street address, because the carrier cannot deliver to a PO box.

## Tracking your order

When your order leaves the warehouse you receive a tracking number by email. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show updates after pickup.

## Lost or delayed packages

If your tracking shows no movement for 5 business days, or the package is marked delivered but you cannot find it, contact us. We start a search with the carrier. If the package cannot be found, we send a replacement or refund the order. Please check with neighbours and around your building first, because carriers sometimes leave packages in a safe place.

## Wrong address

We can change the delivery address only before the order leaves the warehouse. After that, the carrier decides whether it can redirect the package. Check the address carefully at checkout.
