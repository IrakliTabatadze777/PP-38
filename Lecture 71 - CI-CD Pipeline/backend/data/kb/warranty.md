# Product Warranty

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers manufacturing defects: problems caused by how the item was made, such as a seam that comes apart, a zipper that fails under normal use or a tent pole that cracks without being overloaded. It does not cover normal wear and tear, damage from misuse, accidents, improper washing or storage, or modifications.

## Warranty length by product category

Boots and jackets are covered for 1 year from the delivery date. Tents and backpacks are covered for 2 years from the delivery date. Other product categories, such as apparel and accessories, do not have a warranty beyond the return and damaged item policies.

## How a warranty claim works

Contact support with your order number and the email on the order, and describe the defect. Photos help. We check that the item is inside its warranty period and that the problem looks like a manufacturing defect. A member of our support team reviews each warranty claim. If the claim is accepted, we repair the item, send a replacement or refund the amount you paid.

## Warranty and the return window

The warranty is separate from the 30 day return window. After the return window ends, you can still make a warranty claim for a defect if the item is within its warranty period. A claim for a defect after more than 37 days is handled as a warranty claim, not as a return.

## Examples

A tent pole that snaps after 14 months is covered, because tents have a 2 year warranty. A zipper that breaks on a jacket after 14 months is not covered, because jackets have a 1 year warranty. Boot soles that wear down after a season of hiking are normal wear and are not covered.
